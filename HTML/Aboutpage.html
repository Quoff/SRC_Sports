About SRC Sports Academy

Our Mission

To enhance Pentecost University's reputation while promoting sports development within the university and the surrounding community. We strive to create an inclusive environment where students and community members can excel in various sports disciplines.

Our Vision

To evolve into a leading regional sports institution recognized for excellence, innovation, and community engagement. We aim to attract sponsorships, foster external partnerships, and build a strong alumni network.

Funding & Support

The SRC Sports Academy is proudly supported through a combination of university allocations, SRC funds, and generous contributions from local community businesses and national sports bodies. This funding allows us to offer state-of-the-art facilities and expand our programs.
