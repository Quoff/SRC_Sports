Student Success Stories

Kwame Asante — Football

Level 300, BSc Information Technology

Kwame joined the SRC Football Academy in his first year. After two seasons of training and competing at the regional level, he was scouted and signed to a local Premier League development squad — all while maintaining a strong academic record.

Abena Mensah — Gymnastics & Ballet

Level 200, BSc Computer Science

Abena has represented the university at three national-level arts and gymnastics festivals. She now leads student workshops helping first-year students build core strength and discipline through movement.

Emmanuel Ofori — Taekwondo

Level 400, BSc Electrical Engineering

Emmanuel started Taekwondo with zero experience in his first semester. By his final year, he holds a blue belt and competed at the National Open, finishing in the top five nationally in his weight category.
